# RAILS — voice rules for every line of prose

**R11 (ruled, defaults): third-person limited, OC POV, past tense,
clean-and-clear.** Panels (F22) only on update/gain beats — zero lines
otherwise. Figures live in STATUS/panels, never prose.

- Clean and clear: one meaning per clause chain, short sentences at beats,
  no stacked clauses over ~18 words (kit law, guide v2.1).
- Panel lines: `【】`, name = F-series figure or the name STATUS carries
  (F22). Name never invented (STATUS bans).
- No System vocabulary anywhere (PRE4). No [ ] mechanic tags in prose
  (adaptation is felt, not tabulated).
- No rAF dates in prose (k04). No count-numbers in prose (R11).
- Technique growth Low → Mid → High, fusion allowed, never terminal (F14).
- Canon beats quoted-or-free: receipted scenes stay receipted; free dialogue
  around them is free (R1, R7).
- The No-Fix Law (PRE3): no line may fix the future — no prophecies of the
  OC's endpoint, no "he would never again…".
