# ECONOMY — what things cost

Stage 0: skeleton only. Nothing ruled beyond receipts; add-only as rulings
arrive.

- **Village life (receipted context):** Holy Spirit Village, ~300 households,
  farming village south of Nuoding; one blacksmith (Tang Hao) — craft is
  scarce and respected. `[canon]` Douluo ch1.
- **Soul masters' world:** rings come from beasts (age-tiered receipts in
  CANON_GROUND); no ring policy ruled for the OC — nothing to economy yet.
- **No System, therefore:** no shop, no points, no currency of the cheat
  kind (PRE4).
- **Talent economy:** adaptation is free and innate — the serial's real
  currency is *time, danger, and choice*, which are earned on page under
  the No-Fix Law (PRE3). Not a ruling — a description until one is.
