# GLOSSARY — terms as this serial uses them

- **Adaptation Talent** — the OC's womb-born talent (PRE2/PRE4). Never
  named "System" in prose; felt, not tabulated.
- **Ground Fire Dragon Lizard (地火龙蜥-family)** — the OC's martial soul
  (R4, author's exact name). Beast family receipted: Earth Fire Lizard →
  Earth Fire Dragon (10k years) / Earth Fire Scarlet Dragon (mutation) —
  SL4 novel ch154. Ground = Earth (wiki's own alias style).
- **No-Fix Law (PRE3)** — the author's rule: nothing is fixed; things go
  naturally. No ceiling, no endpoint, anywhere in this serial.
- **Holy Spirit Village (圣魂村)** — home village; Tang San's village (R2).
- **Innate soul power** — the awakened reading at day 0. Ours: **mid 5–7**
  (R5); Tang San's: full (canon).
- **Womb talent** — pre-birth origin of the adaptation (PRE2): from birth,
  in the mother's womb.
- **Spine (Lock 4, R12)** — `'______ wants ______ from my protagonist, and
  will ______ to get it.'` **OPEN — the author answered "What"**; re-asked.
- **No System (PRE4)** — no menus, no levels, no skill windows. Ever.
- **Fire-phoenix serial** — the author's SL4 line where a *summoned* Ground
  Fire Dragon Lizard appears (ch50–51). Shared name, zero crossover (R9).
