# BUTTERFLY_EFFECTS — natural consequences only

Pattern learned from `soul_land_4_fire_phoenix` → `codex/BUTTERFLY_EFFECTS.md`
(useful part, adapted).

## Core principle

Natural butterfly effects are **mandatory** — this serial's premise changes
the village years — but they must feel like **consequences of the world**,
not invented ceremonies, fake rewards, or forced set-pieces. Under the
**No-Fix Law (PRE3)**, consequences are earned on page and never
pre-computed into an endpoint.

## Current butterfly state (Stage 0 — before prose)

- **D1 — the friend:** Tang San has no canon childhood friend; the OC is
  one. Canon beats walk untouched (R7); the friendship is the only standing
  divergence. Everything else awaits chapters.
- No other divergence exists. No other divergence may be *claimed* until a
  chapter earns it and logs it here the same turn.

## Banned repair mistakes (learned from fire-phoenix; recorded so they are
never revived here)

- Inventing a formal scene "to prove" the OC matters next to canon (the
  fake-reward pattern the fire-phoenix author rejected).
- Using the OC to replace, outshine, or shortcut a canon beat (R7).
- Ceremony/item/reveal invented to justify a consequence.
- A pre-written villain sentence standing in for organic story pressure —
  the format the author rejected for this serial on 2026-10-03 (R12).

## Next guard
