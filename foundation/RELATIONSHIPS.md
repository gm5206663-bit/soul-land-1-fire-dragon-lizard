## The three leaks that kill a firewall

1. **Suspicion → fact.** Suspecting is not knowing.
2. **Reader-knowledge → character mouth.** The reader's canon memory never
   comes out of a character who has not seen it.
3. **Early gift.** Information arrives no earlier than the earliest valid
   change recorded above.
